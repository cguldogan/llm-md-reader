# AGENTS.md

skill under `.agents/skills/<name>/SKILL.md` — its `references/` pages sit
next to it and are loaded on demand:

- `swiftui-pro` — SwiftUI correctness, modern API, and performance rules
- `swift-concurrency-pro` — structured-concurrency patterns and pitfalls
- `swift-testing-pro` — Swift Testing guidance

Note the deployment target is macOS 15 / Swift 6 mode (`project.yml`); apply
version-gated advice accordingly. Provenance and licenses:
`.agents/skills/README.md`.
